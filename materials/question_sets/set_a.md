# 题目集（共用题库，20题 | 25分钟）

> **难度平衡策略**：4个实验条件使用同一题库，仅打乱题目顺序生成A/B/C/D四个版本。这样难度天然一致，只需在分析中检验顺序效应。

## 数学部分（10题，每题5分）

**M1.** 已知函数 f(x) = x³ - 3x² + 2x - 1，求 f'(1) 的值。

**M2.** 在等差数列 {a_n} 中，a₁ = 3，a₅ = 15，求公差 d。

**M3.** 解方程：2^(x+1) = 32

**M4.** 已知向量 a = (2, -1)，b = (3, 4)，求 a·b。

**M5.** 从 5 名男生和 4 名女生中选 3 人组成小组，求恰好有 2 名女生的概率。

**M6.** 求极限：lim(x→2) (x² - 4)/(x - 2)

**M7.** 已知 sin θ = 3/5，θ 为锐角，求 cos θ。

**M8.** 椭圆 x²/25 + y²/9 = 1 的离心率是多少？

**M9.** 若复数 z = (1+i)/(1-i)，求 |z|。

**M10.** 在 △ABC 中，a=3, b=4, C=60°，求 c。

---

## 英语部分（10题，每题5分）

**E1.** Choose the correct word: The experiment requires careful \_\_\_\_ of variables.
A) manipulation B) manipulate C) manipulative D) manipulated

**E2.** Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A) Neither the students nor the teacher were present.
B) Neither the students nor the teacher was present.
C) Neither the student nor the teachers was present.
D) Neither the student nor the teacher are present.

**E3.** The researcher \_\_\_\_ the data for three hours when the power went out.
A) had been analyzing B) has analyzed C) analyzed D) was analyzed

**E4.** Choose the synonym of "ubiquitous":
A) rare B) pervasive C) unique D) temporary

**E5.** Rewrite in passive voice: "The team conducted the experiment."

**E6.** Identify the error: "Each of the participants have signed the consent form."

**E7.** The findings were \_\_\_\_ significant to warrant further investigation.
A) too B) enough C) sufficiently D) such

**E8.** Choose the best transition: "The sample size was small; \_\_\_\_, the results were promising."
A) moreover B) nevertheless C) consequently D) furthermore

**E9.** Which word is stressed on the second syllable?
A) EXperiment B) hyPOthesis C) VARIable D) PROtocol

**E10.** Paraphrase: "The results indicate a strong correlation between the two variables."
